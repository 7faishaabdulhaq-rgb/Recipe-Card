Pasta

A bowl of cooked pasta with red tomato sauce.

Ingredients:

  1. Pasta
  2. Salt
  3. Olive Oil
  4. Garlic
  5. Tomatoes
  6. Cheese

Instructions:

  1. First, boil water in a big pot.
  2. Add salt and pasta.
  3. Cook until it's soft but not too soft.
  4. Drain the water.
  5. In a pan, add olive oil, garlic and tomatoes.
  6. Cook them together.
  7. Pour it over pasta.
  8. Add grated cheese.

Burger

Easiest burger recipe at home.

Ingredients:

  1. One Burger Bun
  2. Burger Patty
  3. Lettuce Leave
  4. Slice of Cheese
  5. One Slice of Onion
  6. Ketchup
  7. Mayonnaise
  8. Salt
  9. Oil

Instructions:

  1. Heat a pan and add a little oil. Place the burger patty on the pan and cook for about 3 to 4 minutes.
  2. Put a burger bun on a plate.
  3. Spread mayonnaise on the top half of the bun.
  4. Place the cooked burger patty on the bun.
  5. Put a slice of cheese on the burger patty.
  6. Add a lettuce leaf on the top of the cheese.
  7. Sprinkle a pinch of salt.
  8. Add a slice of onion.
  9. Put some ketchup on the top.
  10. Place the top half of the bun on the top to make your burger.
