FUDGE BROWNIE

The perfect fudge & flakey on top Brownie recipe from scratch at home.

Ingredients:

  1. 2 Eggs
  2. 1 cup Powdered Sugar
  3. 1/2 cup Butter
  4. 1/2 cup Flour
  5. 1/3 cup Cocoa Powder
  6. 2 drops of Chocolate Essence
  7. 1 tsp Coffee disolved in 1 tbsp Milk
  8. 1/2 cup Melted Chocolate Chips
  9. 1/8 tsp Salt

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven at 180ºC for 20 minutes.
  2. Grease the pan, keep aside.
  3. Beat eggs untill they are fluffy.
  4. Add sugar.
  5. Keep beating.
  6. Add butter, chocolate essence, melted chocolate chips & coffee mixture.
  7. Keep beating.
  8. Sift flour, cocoa powder & salt.
  9. Add them to the wet mixture and fold. Don't use beater now.
  10. Pour the mixture into the pan and bake at 150ºC for 25 minutes.
